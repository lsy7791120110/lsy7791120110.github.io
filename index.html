Building a route to
nucleoside analogues.

My master's research focused on the synthesis of 2′,2′-difluoro carbocyclic nucleoside analogues. A difficult nucleobase coupling step required comparing reaction conditions and checking what each experiment actually produced.

My role
Performed synthesis, compared coupling conditions, and verified final structures using NMR and mass spectrometry data.
Outcome
Obtained and structurally characterized 10 final analogues.
Approach
Used experimental observations and literature to choose conditions for subsequent synthesis.

Read about my contribution
  • Worked through protection, deprotection, activation and nucleobase coupling steps.
  • Compared bases and an additive after initial coupling conditions did not yield the target product.
  • Recorded conditions and yields, and compared analytical data with the expected structures.

The outcome described here is chemical synthesis and structure confirmation; biological efficacy has not been established.
